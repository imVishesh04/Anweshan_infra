Privacy Policy

ANWESHAN Infra · Last updated: 11 November 2026

ANWESHAN Infra ("we") designs and builds home and commercial interiors and repairs furniture. This page explains what we do with the details you share with us on anweshaninfra.in and on our WhatsApp number.

What we collect

  • Your name and phone number (and your WhatsApp profile name if you message us on WhatsApp).
  • What you tell us about your work: type of work, size of the place, area or city, budget range, timeline, preferred visit time and any message you write.
  • Photos you choose to send us of your place or furniture.
  • Basic technical data needed to keep the website safe, such as your IP address for spam protection.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, call you, plan a site visit and prepare ideas or a quote.
  • To keep a record of enquiries so our team can follow up.

We do not sell your details or share them for advertising. We do not send you marketing messages unless you ask for them.

Where it is kept

Your details are stored securely with the service providers that run our website and messaging: MongoDB Atlas (database), Cloudflare (photos), Railway and Netlify (hosting), Meta (WhatsApp) and Telegram (so the owner gets an alert about your enquiry). Only our team can see your enquiry.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to serve you and for our business records. Unfinished WhatsApp chats are cleared automatically.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your details. On WhatsApp you can also simply stop replying — the chat assistant will not message you on its own.
